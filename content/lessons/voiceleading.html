Voice leading is how each note in a chord moves to the next chord. Smooth voice leading is what makes progressions sound connected rather than blocky.

  • ConventionKeep common tones. If two chords share a note, hold it in the same voice.
  • ConventionMove other voices by the smallest step.
  • Resolve tendency tones: the 7th scale degree (B in C) wants to rise to C; the 7th of a dominant chord (F in G7) wants to fall to E.
  • Contrary motion between bass and melody sounds strong and independent.
  • ConventionClassical style avoids parallel 5ths and octaves between voices. Pop and rock often use them on purpose for a powerful sound.

Common pop texture

Left hand plays roots (single notes or octaves). Right hand plays triads in whichever inversion is closest to the previous chord. Melody sits on top.
