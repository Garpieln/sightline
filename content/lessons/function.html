Every note of a scale can carry a chord built from the scale's own notes. Numbering them lets you think of progressions in any key.

ConventionMost tonal music cycles Tonic → Predominant → Dominant → Tonic. Once you hear a song's chords as functions, you can play it in any key.

Cadences (phrase endings)

  • Authentic V → I: final, conclusive.
  • Plagal IV → I: the "amen" ending, softer.
  • Half ends on V: a comma, asks a question.
  • Deceptive V → vi: sets up an ending, then swerves.
