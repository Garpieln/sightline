A triad stacks two 3rds: root, 3rd, 5th. The quality depends on which 3rds.

QualityRecipe (half steps)On CSound
Major4 + 3C–E–GStable, bright
Minor3 + 4C–E♭–GStable, darker
Diminished3 + 3C–E♭–G♭Tense, wants to resolve
Augmented4 + 4C–E–G♯Unsettled, dreamlike
Sus2 / Sus43rd → 2nd or 4thC–D–G / C–F–GOpen, unresolved

Seventh chords

  • Maj7 (C–E–G–B): lush, jazzy, soft.
  • Dominant 7 (C–E–G–B♭): tension that pulls to the chord a 5th below.
  • Min7 (C–E♭–G–B♭): mellow.
  • Half-diminished (B–D–F–A): the ii chord in minor keys.

Inversions

Same notes, different bottom note. C major: root position C–E–G, 1st inversion E–G–C, 2nd inversion G–C–E. Inversions let you move between chords with tiny hand motions, which is the basis of voice leading.

Try itLeft hand: play C major in root position, 1st and 2nd inversion, up and back down. Do the same for F and G. Then play C (C–E–G) → F (C–F–A) → G (B–D–G) and notice how little your hand moves.
