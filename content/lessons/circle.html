The circle of fifths arranges all 12 keys so that each one is a perfect fifth above the one before it, going clockwise. It's a map of how closely keys and chords are related.

Reading the circle

  • Clockwise adds a sharp. C (none) → G (1♯) → D (2♯) → A (3♯) and so on.
  • Counter-clockwise adds a flat. C → F (1♭) → B♭ (2♭) → E♭ (3♭).
  • The bottom is where they meet. F♯ major (6♯) and G♭ major (6♭) sound identical on a piano.
  • Inner ring: relative minors. Each minor key shares its key signature with the major key beside it. A minor and C major both have no sharps or flats.

Remembering key signatures

  • Sharps are added in the order F C G D A E B ("Father Charles Goes Down And Ends Battle").
  • Flats come in the reverse order: B E A D G C F ("Battle Ends And Down Goes Charles' Father").
  • ConventionSharp keys: the last sharp is a half step below the key's name (F♯ → G major). Flat keys: the second-to-last flat names the key (B♭ E♭ → B♭ major).

Why neighbors sound good together

Keys next to each other on the circle share six of their seven notes. Chords next to each other share notes too, so moving between them sounds smooth and connected.

Every major key's six main chords sit in one wedge of the circle: the key itself, its two neighbors, and the three minor chords inside them. In C major that's F, C, G on the outside (IV, I, V) and Dm, Am, Em on the inside (ii, vi, iii). The seventh chord, B°, is the only one not on the circle.

Moving around the circle

  • ConventionCounter-clockwise is the strong direction for chords. A root falling by a fifth (G → C, D → G, A → D) is the strongest-sounding chord move in tonal music. Progressions like vi–ii–V–I walk counter-clockwise step by step toward home.
  • Clockwise builds tension. I → V moves away from home and sets up a return.
  • Big jumps sound dramatic. Chords far apart on the circle share few notes. That's why borrowed chords like ♭VI and ♭VII sound bold.
  • Key changes to a neighboring key sound natural. Jumps across the circle sound like a dramatic shift.
